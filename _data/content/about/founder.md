Our founder Amy Kapernick grew up in rural Queensland where most people she knew had small businesses. While those businesses started to look into getting themselves online the barriers to entry stop them in their tracks. Something had to change…

Today Amy is a front developer, business owner, [international conference speaker](https://speaking.amyskapers.dev/) and [Microsoft MVP](https://mvp.microsoft.com/en-us/publicprofile/5003595). She regularly shares her knowledge with her peers and the next generation of developers. Through mentoring, coaching, teaching Amy loves feeding into the tech community any way she can. She organises the [DDD Perth](https://dddperth.com/) conference and loves feeding into the tech community any way she can, through mentoring, coaching and teaching. Amy [wears many hats](https://amyskapers.dev), and the one she wears most is making the web work for everyone.
