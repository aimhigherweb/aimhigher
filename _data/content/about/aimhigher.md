AimHigher Web was founded to help bring people and technology together, ensuring that no one gets left out of the web.

We specialise in bespoke front end development and accessibility, from component libraries and code reviews to accessibility reviews and team training. When a project needs more, like design, branding or content, we bring in our vetted industry partners.

You know your product best, and we know the web. Let us help, and leave you to focus on what you know best!
